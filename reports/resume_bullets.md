# Workforce Analytics & Algorithmic Fairness Audit

Python | pandas | scikit-learn | SciPy | statsmodels | Power BI specification

- Built a reproducible workforce analytics pipeline across 1,470 fictional HR records and an independent 16,281-record Adult test set, with schema validation, source hashes and documented data licenses.
- Compared logistic regression and random forest with validation-only model/threshold selection, achieving held-out HR F1 0.505 and recall 0.511 on synthetic attrition labels.
- Audited subgroup selection, error rates and calibration with support counts and Wilson intervals, separating historical income classification from fictional workforce decisions.
